# Measured performance — @mahanaicoach

Source: Viral Finder workspace (companyId 6), Instagram account data pulled
2026-09-07. Sample is **122 published reels, 11 May – 15 Aug 2026**. Every reel
in the set has a stored transcript. Zero carousels had been published at the time
of this analysis, so all carousel guidance is inference from reel demand plus
format mechanics — it is not measured carousel performance. Re-pull and revise
once real carousel numbers exist.

Account at time of pull: 11,571 followers.

## Distribution

| Metric | Value |

| Top 8 posts as share of all views | ~64% |
| Outlier bands | 20 viral · 10 hit · 14 above average · 48 average · 30 below average |

The account is spike-driven. Mining topics that already spiked is a better bet
than testing new ground.

## The strongest lever: "free / open source"

| Group | n | Avg views |
|---|---|---|
| Script mentions "free" or "open source" | 56 | **25,303** |
| Does not | 66 | 6,743 |

~3.75× difference. This is the largest single split in the dataset.

A hard number in the first ~120 characters is a smaller but real lift:
39 posts with one averaged 18,410 vs 83 without at 13,784.

## What dies

| Group | n | Avg views |
|---|---|---|
| Personal / opinion / news openers | 14 | **2,369** |

Includes his ADHD-life posts, "they've lied to us," AI layoff news, "am I the
only one getting these notifications," and day-in-the-life series entries.

**Model drama is a special trap.** Two posts about Sonnet 5 and Fable 5 pulled
27,836 and 28,544 views — but 1.41% and 3.32% engagement, and **52 and 72 saves**.
Reach without retained value. A carousel built on this would fail, because the
format is paid in exactly the thing these posts didn't produce.

## Proven repeatable topic clusters

Ranked by evidence strength, for picking carousel subjects:

1. **Counted agent/skill packs** — cybersecurity 754, SEO 18, ads 6, YouTube 6,
   agency 230. Best saves *and* best comments. Strongest cluster in the account.
2. **Free clone of an expensive SaaS** — Salesforce/CRM 124k, Manus 40k,
   HeyGen 29k, SEMrush/OpenSEO 16k, Higgsfield 13k, CapCut/OpenCut. Reliably
   high views; six separately proven instances available to combine.
3. **Token and cost reduction** — 77% skill 47k, 95% memory skill 32k, 90% CLI
   proxy 7.7k. Three independent wins on one theme.
4. **Lead gen / scraping** — 1,600× scraper 131k, his own Google Maps lead repo
   58k. Strategically the most valuable, because the repo is his.
5. **Resource lists** — every free API 23k, 2.1B free tokens 24k. Purest
   carousel shape available; the reel could only gesture at the list.

## Known gaps

- Instagram data was flagged `stale: true` at pull time; latest synced post was
  15 Aug against a 7 Sep pull date. Unclear whether that reflects a posting gap
  or a sync lag — confirm before treating recent silence as a trend.
- Benchmark against the 5 tracked competitors showed his median at 2,955 vs a
  competitor median of 82,261 across 339 of their reels. Those accounts are
  likely much larger, so read this as directional, not as a like-for-like gap.
- No brand asset file exists. Google Drive holds only 2019–2021 material from a
  previous agency account, unrelated to @mahanaicoach. The Notion "Voice Guide"
  page is an empty placeholder. The design spec in SKILL.md is therefore a
  reasoned default, not his established look.
